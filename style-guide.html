Logo

The wordmark has two halves: green (growth, trust) and tech (energy, action), joined by a sprouting leaf. Always keep all three colors together on a light or deep-green background.

Primary Full color on white. Use it in the header, on invoices and on receipts.

Reversed Full color on Night #14123A. The logo is at its most vivid here. Use it in the hero, the footer and dark banners.

Mono All white. Use it on photos, brand-green fills and single-color prints such as stamps and engraving.

Clear space & minimum size

  • Clear space = the height of the letter "e" on every side. Keep text, edges and other logos out of it.
  • Minimum width: 96px on screen, 25mm in print. Below that, use the leaf mark alone.
  • Favicon / app icon: the leaf sprout on Green 900. See assets/img/favicon.svg.
  • The logo in this prototype is a redrawn vector. Ask GreenTech for the original AI/SVG/EPS file before launch.

Don't

Place it on a mid-tone navy or blue, as the current site header does. The green turns muddy. Use Night instead.

Stretch, squash or rotate it.

Recolor it for a campaign or festival. Change the artwork around it instead.

Place it on orange or a busy photo, where "tech" disappears.

Color

These three brand colors are sampled straight from the logo file. Each has a darker working shade so text and buttons pass WCAG AA contrast.

Green
Brand Green#00A651--green-500

Logo, icons, large headings, fills. For text and buttons, use Green 700 #007A3D.

Orange
Tech Orange#F57F20--orange-500

Urgency: discounts, deals, PC Builder, ratings. Always put dark ink text on it, never white.

Leaf
Leaf#8FC73E--leaf-500

Highlights on dark green, success moments, soft section backgrounds (Leaf 50).

Supporting colors website only, the logo stays the same

On their own, the logo colors feel flat across a whole website. Adding one cool, saturated color gives the green and orange something to stand against, so pages feel alive but still calm. Use it for dark surfaces, links, focus and section accents. Never put it inside the logo.

Indigo
Tech Indigo#4B3FE0--indigo-600

Links, secondary buttons, highlights, charts. White text on it passes AA (6.8:1).

Night
Night#14123A--night-900

Hero, footer and dark sections. Green, orange and leaf are at their most vivid on it.

Voice & content

GreenTech sounds like the helpful technician at the counter: specific, honest about stock, and never pushy. English is the default for UI and specs. Bangla is used for warm, human moments: offers, help and notices.

Do

  • "In stock at Johura Tower. Collect in 1 hour."
  • "Needs a 550W+ power supply."
  • "Call for price. GPU prices change daily."
  • "অর্ডার কনফার্ম হয়েছে! আমরা ১ ঘণ্টার মধ্যে কল করবো।"
  • One hotline everywhere: 01729-080310

Don't

  • "৳0.00" or "Ex Tax: ৳43,000.00"
  • "Best Trusted Retail Computer Store in Bangladesh" (an unprovable claim)
  • A yellow-highlighted notice saying online orders aren't accepted, next to a Buy button
  • Bangla text baked into banner images
  • Twelve phone numbers on one page

Accessibility checklist

Every page and POS screen must pass these checks before it ships.

  • Text contrast is 4.5:1 or higher (3:1 for 24px+ text and icons). Use the pairs in the contrast table.
  • Every image has alt text, and banners carry their message as live HTML text.
  • A visible focus ring (3px orange) on every interactive element. Everything works by keyboard.
  • One <h1> per page, headings in order, and lang="bn" on Bangla text.
  • Stock and sale states use text plus color, never color alone.
  • Layouts work at 360px wide with no horizontal scroll, and tap targets are 36px or larger.
